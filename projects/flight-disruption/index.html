Define the outcome

A disruption is a cancellation, a diversion, or an arrival at least 15 minutes late. Denominators include all departures with a usable outcome.

Split by month

Train on June, set an alert cutoff on July, and evaluate on August. Use only airport, carrier, weekday, scheduled departure hour, distance, and scheduled duration.

Plan a review queue

Use the 80th percentile of July scores as an alert cutoff. Ties and changes in traffic can shift the share of August flights flagged. This is an offline benchmark.
